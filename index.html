The Arcane Chamber

You are sealed in a sorcerer’s vault. Find the way out:

  1. Awaken the enchanted orbs in the order of the rhyme.
  2. Take the brass key that appears on the pedestal.
  3. Attune the three crystals to the banners on the east wall.
  4. Unlock the door and escape.

Desktop: drag to look, WASD to walk, click objects. Headset: use the VR button (bottom right), point with the right controller and pull the trigger.

You Have Escaped

The door sinks into the floor and cool night air rushes in. All four stages are behind you.
