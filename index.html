The Arcane Chamber

You are sealed inside a sorcerer's vault of six chambers. In each, enchanted orbs guard the door — awaken them in the order the old rhyme demands. Beware: not every orb wishes you well.

Desktop: click to look around, WASD to walk, click an orb to awaken it.
Headset: press Enter Realm, then use your controller trigger to select an orb.


You Have Escaped

The last rune fades. Cool night air spills through the final door. All six chambers lie behind you.
